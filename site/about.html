About us

A small studio, built for hands-on delivery

Mishty Web is a digital marketing and web development agency based in Khargone, Madhya Pradesh — built to give local businesses the same digital quality that big-city companies get.

Our story

Started as one developer, one client at a time

Mishty Web began with a simple idea: businesses in Khargone shouldn't have to go to Indore, Bhopal, or Mumbai to get a website and marketing strategy that actually works.

Every project since has been built the same way — hands-on, with the person coding your site being the same person you talk to about your business goals. No agency layers, no relay of information through account managers.

Today that approach has carried us across travel, personal care, recruitment, and manufacturing — proof that the same disciplined process works for any industry.
